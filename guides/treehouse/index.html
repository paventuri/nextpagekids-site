What it’s about

Siblings climb into a tree house, open a dinosaur book and land in prehistoric times. Book one of the Magic Tree House series.

What might come up

A short T. rex chase. It is over fast and nobody gets hurt.

Why kids like it

Short chapters that end on a cliffhanger, plus real dinosaur facts.
