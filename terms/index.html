Personalized reading kits

Each kit is a digital PDF made for your child after you order. We email it within 24 hours to the address you give at checkout. Print as many copies as your family needs. Please don’t resell or share the file.

Refunds

Because every kit is made to order, digital personalized goods can’t be returned. If something is wrong, like a misspelled name we made, a file that won’t open, or a kit that didn’t arrive within 24 hours, email hello@nextpagekids.com and we’ll fix it or refund you.

Family Pass

The Family Pass is a yearly plan that renews until you cancel. You can cancel any time by writing to hello@nextpagekids.com, and you keep the pass until the end of the year you paid for.

Guides and verdicts
